### Why $55K, not the placeholder $50K
**Russell Village = SAME BUILDER (Turquesa) prior platted product**. 8 vacant lots there carry CAD-assessed land of $53K-$71K each (median $55,242). CAD ≈ market for residential in this submarket — calibrated against 7 paired Russell Village improved lots where CAD-gross was 99.6% of recent sold prices. Empirical land share from same data: $55K land / $250K sold = 22% (higher than the 12-18% textbook number for South Texas). Imputed land at 20% of $255K median townhouse retail = $51K, converging on the same ~$50K-$55K floor.

The $65K ceiling captures a wholesale-to-small-builder play (Talgaos retails platted lots to custom builders at premium) but requires utilities + plat completion. Anything north of $70K crosses into Garden Path-level new-construction territory, which assumes Talgaos goes vertical.
