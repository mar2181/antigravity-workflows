## 9. Recommendations

1. **Lock Stonecrest under option NOW** at or below $40K. Do not rely on goodwill.
2. **Day 1 of feasibility:** Pull plat status from City of Edinburg planning. Determine if recorded or preliminary.
3. **Day 1:** Engage RGV civil engineer (Halff/Melden & Hunt) for cost validation. The web-only basis in this memo carries ~±20% uncertainty.
4. **Push contract amendments:**
   - Extend feasibility to 60 days
   - Negotiate 6B(3) survey-update cost to Seller
   - Amend 6A(2) area-and-boundary exception (Buyer-pay, $150–$300)
   - Tie closing to "5 days after Buyer obtains executed Stonecrest contract"
5. **Order ALTA survey** for the easement-burdened north strip.
6. **POF letter to Seller** within 3 days of effective date (no financing contingency means clean cash needs proof).
7. **Plan vertical-build exit strategy** — wholesale numbers do not work; vertical build at $200–230K avg unit yields strong margin.
