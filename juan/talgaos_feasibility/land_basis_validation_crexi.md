**Talgaos blended cost: $102,163/ac** for combined 4.16 ac at $425K total.

Direct comparable (Mission, 8.96 ac, rural-edge): **$100,982/ac active listing** — virtually identical $/ac. Suggests our land basis is **fairly priced for rural-edge RGV land** (away from major arterials).

**Subject standalone: $143,123/ac** ($385K / 2.69 ac) — slightly elevated vs. Mission rural comp; partly justified by Edinburg-specific demand and closer-to-Rogers-Rd location, but on the higher end of the rural range.

**Stonecrest cap: $27,211/ac** ($40K / 1.47 ac) — extremely low. Confirmed by HCAD assessed value of only $10,915. Consistent with the parcel being a bank-owned drainage tract with restricted use. **Buyer should bid $20K, room to $35K** (per Stonecrest unmask brief).
