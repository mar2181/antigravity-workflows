## Layer 2 — Entity Unmask / Successor Trace

**The owner of record is a failed bank.** This requires successor analysis instead of conventional entity unmask.

- **First National Bank** ("FNB-Edinburg"), HQ 100 W. Cano St., Edinburg TX, was closed by the **Office of the Comptroller of the Currency on September 13, 2013**, and the FDIC was appointed Receiver. As of 06/30/2013 FNB had ~$3.1B assets / $2.3B deposits. It was the 22nd US bank failure of 2013 and the largest in 3+ years; the first Texas failure of 2013.
- The FDIC entered into a **Purchase & Assumption (P&A) agreement with PlainsCapital Bank (Dallas, TX)** the same day. PlainsCapital assumed all deposits and acquired ~$2.7B of FNB's assets, including a $1.8B loss-share pool. The 51 former FNB branches reopened the next business day as PlainsCapital branches.
- **PlainsCapital Bank** is a subsidiary of **Hilltop Holdings Inc. (NYSE: HTH)**, headquartered at 325 N. St. Paul St. Suite 800, Dallas TX 75201. It is federally regulated, Member FDIC, in good standing.
- The Stonecrest parcel was almost certainly an **OREO asset** held by FNB at the time of failure (collateral on a foreclosed loan, never resold). Two paths the asset could have taken:
  1. **Most likely (~80%): Acquired by PlainsCapital** as part of the asset purchase, classified as OREO inside PlainsCapital's Special Assets group. The 04/2022 lump-sum tax payment is consistent with PlainsCapital's Special Assets discovering the orphan and clearing the lien risk.
  2. **Possible (~15%): Retained by the FDIC Receiver** and either pooled into a structured FDIC sale or held by FDIC DRR. If so, the FDIC may have separately contracted with a tax servicer.
  3. **Long-tail (~5%): Sold to a third-party OREO buyer** in 2014-2021 via FDIC asset auction, but the buyer never recorded a deed (or recorded one that wasn't reflected in the tax roll). Less likely given the FDIC's careful deed-recording practice.
